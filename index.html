Privacy Policy for Jacket

Last Updated: October 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Jacket (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Jacket helps users decide what to wear based on local weather conditions and personal preferences. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

By using Jacket, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Use

Location Information

Jacket may request access to your device’s location to provide weather recommendations for your current area. Location information is used solely to retrieve local weather conditions and generate recommendations.

Location data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising purposes.

Weather Preferences

Jacket stores preferences you choose during onboarding, such as:

  • Temperature comfort levels
  • Rain preferences
  • Layer preferences
  • Other weather-related settings

These preferences are used to personalize recommendations and may be stored locally on your device.

Manually Entered Locations

If you choose to enter a city manually, the city name is used only to retrieve weather information for that location.

Information We Do Not Collect

Jacket does not collect:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Payment information
  • Government identification information
  • Health information
  • Precise location history
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages

Jacket does not require user accounts.

Third-Party Services

Jacket uses third-party services to provide weather and location information. These services may receive location coordinates or city names solely for the purpose of providing weather forecasts and geolocation data.

Examples may include weather data providers, geocoding providers, and hosting or infrastructure providers. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Data Storage

Most user preferences are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a central database of user profiles.

Children’s Privacy

Jacket is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted within the application or associated website and will include the revised date.
